# Verse Review Guidelines

## Review unit

Review each extracted verse against its exact source revision.
Use another reliable edition when checking suspected source errors.

## Checks

- Verse number and boundaries are correct.
- No lines are missing, duplicated, or merged with another verse.
- Line order and Sinhala joining characters are preserved.
- Headers, categories, and navigation text are excluded.
- Suspicious spelling or symbols are recorded, not silently corrected.

## Decisions

- pending: Not yet reviewed.
- approved: Extraction was checked against the cited source.
- rejected: Extraction is unsuitable; record the reason.

Approval confirms extraction quality, not publication rights.
Rights review is a separate requirement.

## Corrections

Keep the original snapshot unchanged.
Record every correction separately with:
- Source revision and verse number
- Original text and replacement
- Reason and supporting reference
- Reviewer and review date

Do not edit generated candidate files directly.
Do not automatically approve unflagged verses.
